Thin Launcher: a minimalist Android home screen.

Thin Launcher shows only the apps you choose on your home screen. Everything else is one instant search away, so your phone stops pulling you in every time you unlock it.

What Thin Launcher does

  • Only the apps you choose. Your home screen shows just the apps you pick.
  • Instant search. Find any installed app in a tap instead of scrolling pages of icons.
  • Colour themes. Pick a calm palette that suits you, with more on the way.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I download Thin Launcher?

Thin Launcher is on Google Play: Get Thin Launcher on Google Play.

What makes it a "thin" launcher?

It keeps the home screen to the apps you choose and moves everything else behind instant search, so there are fewer icons to distract you.
